P3 ENGINE — SKELETAL ANIMATION DERIVATIONS
============================================================

1. LINEAR BLEND SKINNING (LBS)
----------------------------------------
v' = Σᵢ wᵢ * Mᵢ * v  (linear blend of bone transforms)
  Pros: simple, fast (4x4 matrix multiply)
  Cons: 'candy wrapper' (volume loss on twist),
         intersection on large rotations

2. DUAL QUATERNION SKINNING (DQS)
----------------------------------------
dq = (q_rot, q_dual)  — 8 floats per bone
v' = (Σ wᵢ * dqᵢ / ||Σ wᵢ * dqᵢ||) · v  (after normalization)
  Pros: preserves volume, no candy wrapper, shortest-arc rotation
  Cons: 2x memory, slightly slower

3. FABRIK (Forward And Backward Reaching IK)
----------------------------------------
Each iteration:
  Backward pass: place end-effector at target,
                 adjust each joint to preserve distance to next
  Forward pass: place root at origin,
                adjust each joint to preserve distance to next
Convergence: linear for reachable targets, ~5-20 iters
Cost: O(N) per iteration (N = bones)

4. SLERP (Spherical Linear intERPolation)
----------------------------------------
q(t) = (q_A · sin((1-t)·θ/2) + q_B · sin(t·θ/2)) / sin(θ/2)
  θ = angle between q_A and q_B = arccos(q_A · q_B)
  Used in DQS for shortest-arc rotation blending
