P3 ENGINE — PARTICLE SYSTEM DERIVATIONS
============================================================

1. CUBIC BEZIER LIFE CURVE
----------------------------------------
B(t) = (1-t)³ P0 + 3(1-t)² t P1 + 3(1-t) t² P2 + t³ P3, t ∈ [0,1]
  Used for: intensity, size, color over particle lifetime
  Per-emitter: 4 control points × 3 properties = 12 floats

2. CURL NOISE (turbulence)
----------------------------------------
curl(z) = (∂P/∂y, -∂P/∂x, 0)  where P is Perlin noise potential
  Provides divergence-free (volume-preserving) velocity field
  Used for: smoke, dust, fog particles

3. GRAVITY INTEGRATION
----------------------------------------
Semi-implicit Euler:
  v' = v + a*dt;  p' = p + v'*dt
  Fast, symplectic for short simulations, OK for sparks

Verlet:
  p_new = 2*p - p_prev + a*dt²
  Energy-conserving, better for long simulations (cloth, hair)
